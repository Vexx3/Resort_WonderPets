About Us

Our Story

Isla Serena Beach Resort began in 2015 as a row of five nipa cottages owned by a local family who loved the quiet beauty of San Vicente. Guests kept coming back for the clear water, the long stretch of white sand and the warm welcome, so the family slowly turned their small cottages into the resort it is today.

Isla Serena now has 40 rooms and villas, an infinity pool, a seaside restaurant and a spa, but our goal has stayed the same: to give every guest a peaceful and memorable island stay. Most of our staff come from nearby towns, and we proudly serve fresh food from local fishermen and farmers.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, clean and relaxing beach resort experience with warm Filipino hospitality, while caring for the local community and the environment.

Our Vision

To be one of the most loved family-friendly beach resorts in Palawan, known for its friendly service and its respect for nature.

Our Core Values

  • Hospitality – We treat every guest like family.
  • Respect for Nature – We reduce plastic use, save water and protect our beach and coral reefs.
  • Community – We hire local staff and support local businesses.
  • Excellence – We keep our rooms, food and service at a high standard.
