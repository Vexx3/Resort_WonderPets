Our Amenities

At Isla Serena, there is something for everyone. Swim, dine, relax or explore, all just a few steps from your room. Most of our amenities are free for staying guests, while some activities have a small fee.

Infinity Pool

Our oceanfront infinity pool seems to flow straight into the sea. It has a shallow area for kids and comfortable sun loungers all around it.

Free for room guests

White-Sand Beach

Step out of your room and onto the soft, powdery sand of Long Beach. Beach umbrellas, lounge chairs and towels are available all day.

Free for room guests

Seaside Restaurant & Bar

Our open-air restaurant serves fresh seafood, classic Filipino dishes and cold drinks. Join us every Saturday night for our beach barbecue.

Breakfast included in all room rates

Serenity Spa

Unwind in our open-air spa pavilion surrounded by tropical plants. Choose from traditional hilot, Swedish and hot stone massages.

Starts at ₱800 per hour

Water Sports & Island Hopping

Paddle a kayak, go snorkeling over colorful coral reefs or join our island-hopping tour to nearby islands and hidden beaches.

Kayak rental starts at ₱300 per hour

Hammock Garden

Find a quiet spot in our garden of palm trees and hammocks. It is the perfect place to read a book, take a nap or watch the waves.

Free for all guests
