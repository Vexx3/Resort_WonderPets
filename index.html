Welcome to Isla Serena!

Isla Serena Beach Resort is a peaceful beachfront getaway in San Vicente, Palawan. It sits right along Long Beach, one of the longest white-sand beaches in the Philippines. Our resort is the perfect place to slow down, breathe in the fresh sea air and enjoy the simple beauty of island life.

Whether you are planning a family vacation, a romantic escape for two or a fun weekend with friends, our friendly staff will make you feel right at home. Spend your days swimming in our infinity pool, kayaking in clear blue water or relaxing in a hammock under the palm trees. In the evening, enjoy fresh seafood at our seaside restaurant while the sun sets over the sea.
