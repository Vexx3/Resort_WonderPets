Room Rates

Choose from four room types made for every kind of traveler. All rooms have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a private bathroom with hot and cold shower, and free breakfast.

Garden Standard Room

A cozy room with a queen-size bed and a view of our tropical garden. Ideal for couples or solo travelers.

Starts at ₱3,500 per night

Deluxe Ocean View Room

Wake up to the sound of the waves. This room has a king-size bed and a private balcony facing the sea.

Starts at ₱5,500 per night

Family Suite

A spacious suite with two queen-size beds and a sitting area. Great for families with kids.

Starts at ₱8,500 per night

Beachfront Villa

Our best accommodation, with two bedrooms, a kitchenette and a private plunge pool just steps from the beach.

Starts at ₱15,000 per night

Important Notes

  • Check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 12:00 NN.
  • An extra person costs ₱800 per night, including an extra mattress and breakfast.
  • Children 6 years old and below stay for free when sharing the existing beds.
  • Weekend and holiday rates also apply on Philippine public holidays, during Holy Week and from December 20 to January 3.
  • Rates may change without prior notice.
